Hour Maker

A Business Pitch By Edward Carroll

What am I trying to make? A n app to help young students find their first job.

Business Name: Hour maker

I chose the name Hour Maker as it captures the core idea of the business: to help young people get their first working hours. The name is short, simple and easy to remember and can easily be passed down from student to student.

Hour Maker is a job-finding service for young students that have never worked. Existing platforms like SEEK can overwhelm first-time jobseekers with roles that require experience, Hour Maker filters listings to show opportunities specifically for inexperienced applicants.

(Australian Bureau of Statistics. , 2026) National population estimate (13-16 years).

(Australian Bureau of Statistics. , 2026) Population estimates for Queensland (13-16 years).

Using data from the AKG First Job to Future Career Report Australia (n.d.), 78% of young people do not have much knowledge of local job opportunities. Hour Maker seeks to tap into this opportunity.

Target Market

The ideal customer s are 13-16 year old school students in the Wynnum area who are seeking jobs but don’t have any experience and are interested in building their skills while earning money. They would buy Hour Maker because it replaces both applying in-store and scouring the internet with verified, entry level listings browsable in minutes. Nationally, a n estimated 1.362 million people fit this demographic, a market Hour Maker could reach if scaled (Australian Bureau of Statistics. , 2026). Digital marketing through platforms like TikTok and Instagram will reach this audience effectively, as these are popular platforms with this demographic.

Product:

Solves the issue of young people finding it difficult to find jobs

Stands out as it is directed specifically at young people with little/no experience

Not part of a product range or ecosystem

New product, new service

Features including verified listings and beginner-friendly filters addressing needs for safety and simplicity.

Price

Will start as a one time purchase of $20. While Seek may be free we are directly going into a niche market and Seek makes money from companies paying for advertising roles on seek. Using a launch model of $20 means that it is affordable for my target audience (teenagers that have no jobs, thus no real disposable income), and a potential future pricing model similar to that used by Seek (free for users, employers pay for advertising) could be used when Hour Maker is properly scaled with a large user base.

The price is justifiable as it targets a niche that isn’t met by free platforms and has the potential to be more flexible in the future.

Place

Online product to reach as many people as possible, although as a startup focusing on the Wynnum/Wider Wynnum area will be preferred.

Starting in Wynnum allows for controlled testing before being scaled up.

Promotion

Will promote in schools first and online using popular social media sites with young people, eg TikTok, Instagram as they are cheap and easy to mass produce. Promoting the website using google promotions may also be beneficial when people search up “seek” or “first job listings” etc.

School based promotions provide immediate access to the target audience.

Hour Maker has strong potential as it fills a gap in the market: 78% of young people lack knowledge of local job opportunities. The biggest growth opportunity is scaling up from Wynnum to other regions through school-based advertising, as schools give access to hundreds of potential customers at once. The greatest challenge will be compliance with local, state and federal minor-protection laws, which is why verified listings will be built in from the start. Investors should support Hour Maker as it combines a proven demand, a constantly renewing market, a clear path for scalability and a defensible niche that major competitors like Seek and Indeed have not attempted to fill.
